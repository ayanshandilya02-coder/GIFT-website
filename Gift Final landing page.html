About Us

A studio, not a lecture hall:

Redefining Design Education in Northeast India


At GIFT (Guwahati Institute of Fashion Technology), we believe design isn’t memorised—it’s created. We have swapped traditional lecture halls for immersive, industry-grade creative studios right here in Guwahati, Assam. As a pioneer design college under prestigious Gauhati University, our Bachelor of Design (B.Des) programmes offer an experimental, project-based learning ecosystem. Here, theory serves practice, and students work alongside industry professionals to transform raw concepts into market-ready innovations

As the best fashion design institute in Guwahati, our university-affiliated degree programs bridge North East India's rich heritage with global market trends. Whether you are enrolling in our premier B.Des Fashion & Textile Design program or pursuing a career through our industry-focused B.Des Interior Design course, our campus serves as a launching pad for future-proof careers. Structured in alignment with the National Education Policy (NEP 2020), we provide hands-on experience through regular market visits, industrial tours, and live fashion shows. By combining studio-based learning with robust 100% placement assistance and elite internship opportunities, GIFT transforms raw creative passion into thriving, employable professions.

  • Industry-practising faculty across both departments
  • In-house tailoring, textile and 3D visualisation labs
  • Annual graduate showcase open to the public and press

Fashion Designing

From first sketch to final stitch.

Our fashion design track covers everything between a sketchbook idea and a garment on a runway: draping, pattern-making, construction, textiles, and collection storytelling.

Students work in fully equipped studios with industrial machines, and every term ends with a critique from practising designers rather than a written exam alone.

  • Garment construction & draping studios
  • Textile, print and embroidery labs
  • Mentorship from working designers
  • Annual graduate runway showcase

Interior Designing

Space is the material you shape.

The interior design track blends spatial planning, materials and lighting with the software skills studios actually use, so students graduate able to take a brief from concept to a construction-ready drawing set.

Live briefs with real clients — cafés, clinics, small retail spaces — run alongside the core coursework each year.

  • CAD, SketchUp and rendering workshops
  • Materials, lighting and sustainability modules
  • Live client briefs from year two onward
  • Site visits to active design projects
